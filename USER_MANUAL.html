1. Overview & Key Capabilities

Senang Member App is a modern, cross-platform membership application designed for salon, beauty, wellness, and retail clients. Built with .NET Blazor and MAUI, it delivers a smooth native experience on mobile devices and web browsers.

Multi-Merchant Account

Switch between affiliated salons, clinics, or stores without maintaining separate accounts.

Interactive Service Booking

Reserve slots by choosing outlets, service categories, staff specialists, and live time slots.

AI Chat & Voice Assistant

Ask questions, schedule bookings, or inquire about remaining balances using natural text or voice.

Prepaid & Package Tracking

Keep track of cash credits, prepaid package sessions, and reward points in real time.

2. Getting Started & Authentication

Account Registration

  1. Launch the application and tap Register.
  2. Provide your Full Name, Mobile Phone Number, Email Address, and Password.
  3. Tap Submit to create your membership profile.

Signing In

  1. Enter your registered Mobile Phone Number on the login screen.
  2. Type your Password and tap Log In.
  3. Your session is securely cached so you stay logged in.

Password Recovery

If you have forgotten your password, tap Forgot Password? on the login screen, enter your phone number, verify the SMS OTP (One-Time Password), and specify your new password.

3. Merchant & Outlet Selection

The app supports multi-merchant memberships. If you visit multiple affiliated branches or sister brands:

  • Tap the Merchant Name banner located at the top of the Home screen or via Profile > Switch Merchant.
  • Select the desired store from the list.
  • All balances, announcements, catalog items, and booking options will immediately refresh for that store.

4. Home Dashboard Overview

The Home dashboard provides an executive overview of your membership:

  • Balance Summary Cards: Direct view of your available Credits, active Packages, Loyalty Points, and Vouchers.
  • Quick Actions: One-tap shortcuts to Book Appointment and launch the AI Assistant.
  • Announcements Carousel: Important promotions, news updates, and holiday notices from the merchant.
  • Next Upcoming Appointment: A dedicated card displaying your nearest scheduled appointment, location, date/time, and assigned specialist.

5. Service & Product Catalog

Explore all treatments, packages, and retail items offered by your merchant:

  1. Tap the Catalog tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  2. Select a category (e.g., Hair Care, Facial Spa, Nail Art, Wellness).
  3. Browse products and services with high-resolution imagery and descriptions.

Step 1: Select Outlet

Choose the branch location where you would like to receive the service, then tap Next.

Step 2: Select Services

  • Category Filter: Tap category pills (All, Hair, Nails, etc.) to browse filtered services.
  • Search: Use the search bar to find services by name or description.
  • Multi-Selection: Tap any service card to select/deselect it.
  • Details Modal: Long-press a card to inspect its full description, category, and brand details.

Step 3: Select Staff Specialist

Pick your preferred stylist or specialist, or select Any Staff / Online if you have no preference.

Step 4: Select Date & Time Slot

Navigate the weekly calendar and pick an available time slot (e.g., 10:30, 14:00). Past or booked slots are automatically disabled.

Step 5: Review & Confirm

Review all appointment details (Branch, Selected Services, Specialist, Scheduled Time, and optional Notes/Memo). Tap Confirm Booking to finalize your appointment.

7. Managing Appointments

  • View Scheduled Appointments: Open the Booking tab to toggle between 1-Month View and 1-Year View.
  • Directions & Contact: Tap an appointment card to view branch address, contact details, and open Google Maps / Waze.
  • Cancellation: Open the appointment details, tap Cancel Appointment, and confirm to release your reserved slot.

8. AI Chatbot Assistant

Tap the AI Assistant icon on the Home page for an interactive concierge experience:

  • Natural Language Chat: Ask questions like "What are your opening hours?" or "Can I book a facial this Saturday at 2 PM?".
  • Voice Recording: Hold the microphone button to speak naturally.
  • In-Chat Booking: The assistant can guide you and lock in your appointment without leaving the conversation.

9. Member Balances & Wallet

Wallet Item Description Usage
Prepaid Credits Cash balance stored in your membership account. Redeemable directly during in-store checkout.
Service Packages Purchased treatment bundles (e.g., 5x Scalp Treatment). Deducted per completed session.
Loyalty Points Reward points accumulated from transactions. Convertible to cash rebates and discounts.
Vouchers Promotional or birthday discount coupons. Apply during service payment.

10. Purchase History & Receipts

Review complete service histories and purchase receipts by navigating to the History tab:

  • Filter transactions by year and month.
  • View detailed itemized receipts including quantities, staff specialist name, and branch.
  • Tap the review button to rate your experience and provide direct feedback.

11. Profile & Settings

Tap the Profile tab to access settings:

  • Edit Profile: Update your name, email, birthday, and gender.
  • Change Password: Update your login password securely.
  • Language Switcher: Switch between English, Bahasa Malaysia, and 简体中文 (Chinese).
  • Account Deletion: Permanently remove your account and personal data.
  • Logout: Safely end your session.

12. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I open this manual in Google Docs?

Go to docs.google.com > Click the Open file picker (Folder icon) > Upload > Select this USER_MANUAL.html or USER_MANUAL.md file.

Q: Can I book for multiple services in one session?

Yes. In Step 2 (Select Services), tap each service you require. The app will tally all selected items and compute the total estimated duration.

Q: What if no slots are available for my preferred stylist?

You can choose Any Staff / Online in Step 3, or select another date on the calendar.
